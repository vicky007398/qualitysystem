Inspection Modules

Built around the checksheets your quality team uses.

Detailed checksheet fields support consistent record keeping across inspections.

In-Process Quality

IPQC Checksheet

  • Detailed in-process quality checks.
  • Inspection context such as date, shift, line, and inspection time.
  • Production and material details where applicable.
  • Structured observations across manufacturing stages.
  • Operator submission and incharge review.
Includes stage observations & review workflow
Critical to Quality

CTQ Test

  • Critical-to-quality process parameter checks and sample readings.
  • Calculated results based on entered measurements.
  • Review, approval, and PDF output.
Calculates results from entered CTQ readings
Incoming Quality

IQC Test

  • Structured incoming material inspection and test readings.
  • Batch, lot, and specification checks with calculated outputs where applicable.
  • Review, approval, and PDF output.
Structured IQC records with approval & PDF export
How It Works

A simple flow from inspection to approval.

  1. 01 Operator fills checksheet
  3. 02 Submits report
  5. 03 Shift incharge reviews
  7. 04 Approves report
  9. 05 Approved PDF available

Operator

Individual Login
  • • Signs in with a separate login ID and password.
  • • Enters inspection readings and saves work in draft.
  • • Submits completed reports for review.

Shift Incharge

Individual Login
  • • Signs in with a separate login ID and password.
  • • Reviews submitted inspection details and records a review decision.
  • • Approves reports after checking.

Admin

Dashboard & Analysis
  • • Dedicated admin login with access to the full dashboard and attached analysis screen.
  • • Oversees inspection records, submission status, and approval activity.
  • • Manages individual user credentials and permissions.
