BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few useful answers.

Do we need to change our checksheets?

The demo retains the original IPQC and CTQ fields, tables, sample counts, and calculations. Review the forms with your quality team during a pilot to confirm they match your controlled process.

Who can see and approve records?

Operators manage their own entries. The assigned shift incharge reviews submitted reports; administrators can monitor all reports and also review pending records. A separate Super User manages accounts.

Can we download reports?

Yes. Individual report PDFs and daily PDF downloads are available according to the signed-in user's access.

Can we use it without internet?

The hosted demo needs a connection to save and review records. The app can also run on a company server; offline entry and automatic syncing are not currently supported.

How should we start?

Begin with one line or shift. Verify form coverage, train operators and reviewers, and measure record completeness, approval time, and retrieval time. Production use also needs secure accounts, persistent storage, and backups.
